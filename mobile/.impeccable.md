## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are industrial safety officers, site supervisors, and compliance inspectors in construction, manufacturing, logistics, and other high-risk environments. They use the app on active sites and factory/warehouse floors while moving through noisy, time-constrained, safety-critical conditions.

### Brand Personality
Clear, authoritative, efficient. The interface should communicate trust, fast situational awareness, and operational clarity under pressure.

### Aesthetic Direction
Pragmatic, high-clarity safety tooling with strong visual legibility in mixed indoor/outdoor lighting conditions. Prioritize readable overlays and immediate status comprehension over decorative UI.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize instant comprehension of compliance state in the field.
2. Keep interaction friction low for one-hand, in-motion use.
3. Preserve high contrast and readable typography in variable lighting.
4. Surface errors and recovery paths clearly and quickly.
5. Favor reliable, consistent behavior over ornamental complexity.
